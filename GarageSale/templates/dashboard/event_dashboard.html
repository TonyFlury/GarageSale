What’s being counted?

Timing & data quality

Application counts start at each event’s own booking opening date and use the specific application timestamp, rather than when the location was created. Currently inactive or deleted applications are excluded. Dates after the snapshot or event day are excluded.

Contributions start at the event’s “use from” date and are dated by the contributor’s latest event-specific Confirmed status. Unconfirmed contributions are excluded. Confirmation measures a commitment, not a payment received.

Unknown dates or amounts are flagged. A campaign with incomplete history is excluded from dated comparisons, not extended as a fabricated curve. A genuinely completed, shorter campaign stays in the comparisons at its final total. Final comparisons still include totals whose dates are unknown, when amounts are known.

Weekly additions include day 0 separately, complete seven-day intervals and a labelled partial final interval. Averages and maxima are calculated from each event’s additions for the same interval.
